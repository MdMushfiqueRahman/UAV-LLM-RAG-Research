Experiment ID: EXP-002
Experiment Date: 2026-10-28
Model: GPT-6 (ChatGPT Temporary Chat)
Dataset: UAV Attack Dataset
Scenario: GPS Spoofing
Input: First 10 malicious-labeled telemetry records
Ground Truth: malicious
LLM Prediction: Suspicious
Suspicious-Behavior Confidence: Medium
Confirmed Attack: No
RAG Enabled: No

Evaluation:
- The model identified potentially suspicious telemetry.
- Under the provisional mapping of Suspicious to
  malicious, the prediction is a true positive.
- The model did not confirm a cybersecurity attack.

Observed Evidence:
- Jamming indicator increased from 40.1 to 41.0.
- Satellites used decreased from 10.83 to 9.33.
- Reported velocity increased from 0.146 to 1.139.
- Latitude and longitude remained nearly constant.
- GPS fix type remained at 3.

Research Limitations:
- Single 10-record window.
- Single model response.
- The sample comes immediately after the label transition.
- Possible interpolation and preprocessing effects.
- Feature units and thresholds are not fully verified.
- No repeated trials or controlled API configuration.
- No generalizable detection accuracy established.

Conclusion:
GPT-6 classified this malicious-labeled telemetry
window as Suspicious. This is one preliminary observation
and does not establish reliable intrusion detection.
