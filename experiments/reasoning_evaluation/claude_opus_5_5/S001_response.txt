# Telemetry Analysis: Sample S001

## 1. Classification

**Insufficient Evidence**

The window shows no clear signs of malicious activity, and its behavior is broadly consistent with a stable, near-stationary platform. However, the window is short, there is no baseline, and the meanings and units of key fields (especially `jamming_indicator`) are not defined. The data does not support a definitive Benign or Malicious label.

## 3. Reasoning

**Observations that do not suggest interference:**
- The satellite count and fix type are completely stable.
- Position and altitude change only by tiny amounts.
- There are no jumps, dropouts, or discontinuities in position, altitude, or velocity.
- The velocity field stays near zero, consistent with the very small positional changes.

Taken together, the data is internally consistent with a platform holding nearly still.

**Observations that cannot be interpreted without more context:**
- **`jamming_indicator`:** Its scale, units, and normal range are not provided. A value of 47 cannot be judged high or low without a baseline. The rise from 47.0 to 47.4 is small and gradual, but its significance cannot be assessed from this window.
- **Uniform step patterns:** The constant altitude and velocity increments occur regardless of the irregular time spacing. This is an observable regularity. It could reflect how the data was logged, resampled, interpolated, or merged. It is not, by itself, evidence of manipulation.
- **Limits of stability as evidence:** A stable, consistent stream does not rule out an attack that produces plausible values. Equally, there is no supplied evidence that such an attack occurred. Stability alone cannot confirm either case.

**Why not Malicious:** No observation directly indicates an attack. There are no abrupt position shifts, no loss or degradation of fix, no satellite count anomalies, and no inconsistency between velocity and position.

**Why not Benign:** The window is short, there is no reference baseline or ground truth, and `jamming_indicator` is uninterpretable. These gaps prevent a confident benign determination. The evidence leans toward normal behavior, but not definitively.
